2. HPO Adapter Damage Hypothesis

Strong signal: xLSTM base-only held-out test heads beat xLSTM HPO-adapter validation heads by a large margin: SIB macro F1 0.7022 versus 0.2639; NER F1 0.4155 versus 0.0155.

This does not prove the adapter weights are intrinsically harmful in every use. It does show that the selected generative LoRA/adaptation path hides or damages signal that the base exposes under a small supervised head.
