Latest Architecture Research for SALLM

Fresh literature pass on 2026-06-21 for a South African low-resource architecture-comparison dissertation. Primary sources only where possible: papers, official repos, model cards, and release notes.

Updated answer: the best dissertation lineup is still a controlled comparison of LLaMA-style Transformer, Mamba-family efficient recurrence, and xLSTM, with HGRN2/BabyHGRN as the strongest next low-resource candidate. The newest papers make xLSTM more important, not less. Gated DeltaNet should be a bounded hybrid screen, and M2RNN / TTT / Titans should be cited as emerging future work unless implementation becomes cheap.

What Changed Since The Earlier Plan

  • xLSTM moved up. ICLR 2026 scaling-law work reports favorable scaling from 80M to 7B and 2B to 2T tokens. A June 2026 subquadratic comparison finds xLSTM stronger than Mamba-2 and Gated DeltaNet across code pretraining, distillation, and time-series settings.
  • GDN is still interesting, but mainly as hybrid. GDN-2, Qwen3-Next, and Kimi Linear all strengthen the GDN/KDA family, but the strongest practical designs keep attention or sliding-window attention around for recall.
  • Low-resource evidence still favors HGRN2/BabyHGRN. BabyHGRN remains the cleanest direct sample-efficiency result. The 2025 BLaLM paper adds another low-resource signal: mLSTM or linear attention plus local attention and careful optimization can beat small Transformer baselines.
  • New wildcards are real but expensive. M2RNN, TTT, and Titans are worth citing, but not worth derailing the dissertation unless their code drops cleanly into SALLM.

Best Research Design

  1. Do not chase architectures one by one forever. The thesis contribution is the controlled South African-language comparison, not a leaderboard of every 2026 sequence mixer.
  2. Use the same corpus, tokenizer, parameter scale, train budget, and downstream gates. Otherwise the architecture claim becomes mush.
  3. Separate base quality from task adaptation. Keep base validation loss, clean conditional generation loss, source preservation, POS/NER constrained scoring, and downstream chrF/F1 as distinct evidence.
  4. Make source preservation a first-class metric. Latest literature keeps warning that fixed-state models struggle with copying and retrieval. That maps directly to T2X and AfriHG.
  5. Prefer bounded screens before full runs. A 10K-ish screen plus clean-loss/source-preservation probes should gate HGRN2, GDN hybrid, Mamba-3, or M2RNN before any full pretraining spend.

Decision

The best possible research path is not "train the newest thing." It is: finish LLaMA vs Mamba vs xLSTM cleanly, add HGRN2 as the strongest low-resource missing candidate, then use GDN hybrid as a focused test of the Mamba source-preservation hypothesis. Cite Mamba-3, GDN-2, Kimi Linear, Qwen3-Next, M2RNN, TTT, and Titans to show the dissertation is current, but do not let them explode the experimental matrix.
