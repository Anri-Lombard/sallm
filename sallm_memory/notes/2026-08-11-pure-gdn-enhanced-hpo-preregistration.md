## Frozen Stage-B search

The canonical registry is
`src/conf/hpo/pure_gdn_enhanced_v1.json`. It contains eight scrambled Sobol
points generated by PyTorch `SobolEngine(dimension=4, scramble=True,
seed=20260811)`. The transforms are:

- learning rate: log-uniform from `2e-5` through `2e-4`;
- LoRA rank: equal-width categorical bins mapping to `{8, 16, 32}`;
- LoRA alpha: exactly `2 * rank`;
- LoRA dropout: uniform from `0.0` through `0.1`;
- warmup ratio: uniform from `0.01` through `0.1`.

The registry stores the raw Sobol coordinates and the full-precision
transformed values. Stage B evaluates `b0` through `b7` once at selection seed
`42`. The original Stage-A `a0` through `a2` configurations are also stored in
the registry, yielding eleven candidates per family.

The registry and ranking implementation are architecture-neutral. The same
candidate budget, seed policy, validation-only ranking, confirmation rule,
and tie breaks are the uniform SALLM process for pure GDN, LLaMA, Mamba,
xLSTM, and the historical Qwen-GDN hybrid. Each architecture/family run must
still be preregistered and executed independently against its own frozen base
checkpoint and structurally valid LoRA target modules; results from one model
never choose another model's candidate. The current submission scope remains
pure GDN only.

Everything else remains frozen from the corrected validation protocol:
canonical pure-GDN base, architecture-complete LoRA targets, task data,
prompt/template contract, label-smoothed causal shift, AdamW betas and weight
decay, cosine scheduler, effective batch construction, maximum epochs,
validation metrics, coverage assertions, early-stopping patience `2`, absolute
threshold `0.001`, checkpoint retention, BF16, and no adapter merge. Stage B
uses validation only and cannot submit held-out evaluation.

No ASHA/Hyperband pruning is introduced. Corrected NER curves show that the
primary generated validation metric can improve late, and the literature
warns that low-fidelity rank crossings can discard the eventual winner.
Within-run preregistered early stopping remains the only pruning rule.

## Stage-B ranking and Stage-C confirmation

After all eleven candidates for a family terminate with valid coverage and
artifacts, rank them by the family's existing primary validation metric at
seed `42`. Greater is better except for General equal-family assistant-token
NLL, where lower is better.

Take the leading two candidates to Stage C. Reuse their seed-42 Stage-A or
Stage-B result and run exactly two additional validation-only replications at
seeds `13` and `87`. Rank the two candidates by the arithmetic mean over seeds
`13`, `42`, and `87`. Report the three values and their sample standard
deviation; seeds are replications and the best individual seed is never
selected.

Exact mean ties prefer lower LoRA rank, then lower learning rate, then the
lexicographically earlier candidate ID. Each seed selects its checkpoint only
with the already frozen within-run validation metric and checkpoint tie rule.

The final family recipe is the Stage-C winner. Its seed-42 retained checkpoint
is the frozen production adapter for the later one-time held-out evaluation.
No held-out score may change the candidate, seed, checkpoint, retry, prompt,
or decoding contract.

## Execution, failure, and reporting gates

- Use only A100-40GB `gpu:ampere`, at most three owned jobs, with
  `nlpgroup/a100/nlpgroup`, one GPU, 24 hours, eight CPUs, and the canonical
  working directory. Do not overlap A100-80GB or L40S work.
- Submit Stage-B jobs incrementally as slots become available; do not create a
  large pending array. Preserve every success and failure artifact.
- Infrastructure failures may receive one unchanged retry under a new output
  tag only when no metric was produced or consulted. Recipe/configuration
  failures remain evidence and are not silently replaced.
- Every run must create and verify a complete source/config/environment/model
  execution manifest from a new immutable read-only deployment.
- Before ranking, require declared/evaluated coverage equality, finite primary
  metrics, exact registry/config reconciliation, and artifact hashes.
- Report the full search space, all trials, failures, runtime/GPU hours,
  validation trajectories, seeds, mean and variation, and selection rule.
- The larger pure-GDN budget does not make historical cross-architecture
  comparisons controlled. Architecture-superiority claims require matched HPO
  budgets or an explicit comparability caveat.
- Multilingual winners remain scientifically unfrozen until Stage C completes
  for all eight families. Monolingual selection, held-out adapter tests, Sheet
  E/F/G publication, and Hugging Face publication remain blocked until the
  existing downstream gate is satisfied.
