## Architecture crossover root-cause analysis

- Consolidated xLSTM, LLaMA, Mamba, and corrected GDN mono/multilingual
  findings.
- xLSTM NER/POS discrepancy is substantially explained by evaluation
  formulation: xLSTM has strong token-level NER representations but fails at
  free-form span copying and formatting; constrained token-label POS avoids
  that bottleneck.
- xLSTM Injongo multilingual strength is plausible and supported by controlled
  task-head results, but old LLaMA/Mamba generative comparisons are not
  protocol matched. Matched evaluation must precede parity HPO.
- AfriHG xLSTM-over-LLaMA multilingual differences are small or
  checkpoint/decoding sensitive and are not yet a robust architecture result.
- T2X failure is primarily source entity/value preservation, motivating a
  placeholder-and-reinsertion rescue rather than more ordinary HPO.
- Corrected GDN results disprove a general multilingual advantage: Injongo is
  chance for both regimes, MasakhaNews is identically collapsed, while
  multilingual AfriHG and NER exceed mono. Audit exposure and adapter loading
  before GDN HPO.
