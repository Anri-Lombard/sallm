## GDN evaluation root-cause audit

- Found that lm-eval was scoring `[EOS]` instead of the first continuation
  token because the SALLM tokenizer added BOS/EOS to both separately encoded
  context strings. In GDN News, all 948 English and 297 Xhosa examples in
  every prompt had four-way ties among one-token labels and therefore defaulted
  to the first choice, `business`.
- Found a second News-specific error: reused Mamba configs forced untied
  embeddings, causing Transformers to initialize a random GDN `lm_head` after
  adapter merge.
- Confirmed adapters are nonempty and learned: 2,658,208 trainable parameters,
  nonzero LoRA B tensors, decreasing Injongo/News validation loss, and
  successful private Hub pushes.
- Fixed the shared lm-eval model args with `add_bos_token=false`; GDN launchers
  now force tied embeddings. Tests and shell syntax checks passed. A live News
  canary loaded without a random-head warning and produced unique label scores.
- Stopped the invalid POS pass, preserved its files, and restarted a fresh GDN
  adapter matrix in `_r3` directories. SIB-all is active on Kombuys GPU 0.
  Completed AfriHG `_r2` results remain valid because they use the separate
  native generation evaluator and are skipped by the rerun.
- Do not use `_r2` lm-eval classification metrics or launch HPO from them.
  Full details: `sallm_gdn_eval_boundary_audit_2026-07-17.md` in Obsidian.
