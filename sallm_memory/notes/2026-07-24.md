## AfriHG truncation audit - approximately 06:35 SAST

- The suspected left-truncation explanation was checked against the actual
  code and live LLaMA configuration. `_configure_task_truncation` applies
  left truncation only to `CLASSIFICATION`; AfriHG is `INSTRUCTION`, so both
  corrected GDN and the matched LLaMA control still use the tokenizer default
  `truncation_side=right` with `max_length=1024`. The current collapse was
  therefore not caused by the classification left-truncation change.
- A validation-only 64-example audit found prompt lengths from 129 to 3,240
  tokens (median 565.5). Five of 64 prompts exceed the 1,024-token training
  window and would lose the complete assistant headline under right
  truncation; two exceed the 2,048-token generation context and are
  left-truncated by the generation evaluator. The other 59 examples still
  collapse, so truncation cannot be the sole explanation.
- A full train/validation length audit using only the cached AfriHG
  train/dev CSVs found that right truncation places the complete answer beyond
  the 1,024-token window for 906/24,649 training rows (`3.68%`) and
  118/3,082 validation rows (`3.83%`). Some truncation occurs for
  938/24,649 training rows (`3.81%`). Only 16/24,649 train prompts (`0.06%`)
  and 7/3,082 validation prompts (`0.23%`) exceed the 2,048-token generation
  boundary.
- Interpretation: instruction right truncation is a genuine supervision
  defect and train/generation mismatch that should be repaired before final
  AfriHG training, but its measured prevalence and collapse on short examples
  rule it out as the complete root cause. A minimal matched left-versus-right
  validation control can estimate its contribution; optimization/objective
  and capacity/task difficulty remain live hypotheses.
- Cross-task scope: corrected News configurations are `CLASSIFICATION`, so
  they receive left truncation and preserve the terminal class label.
  Historical News adapters trained before that repair used right truncation;
  at the 1,024-token cap this was already measured to remove complete labels
  from at least 830/4,341 examples (`19.1%`) under the shortest prompt.
  T2X is `INSTRUCTION` and therefore still selects right truncation, but a
  full cached train/validation audit found no actual exposure: the longest
  complete train example is 229 tokens and the longest validation example is
  215, well below both the 1,024-token LLaMA/xLSTM and 2,048-token Mamba
  limits. The truncation defect is therefore material for AfriHG and
  historical News, but not T2X.
