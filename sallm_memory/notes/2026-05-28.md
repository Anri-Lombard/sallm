## 2026-05-28 - advisor architecture note: GatedDeltaNet

- Advisor pointed to arXiv `2507.06457`, "A Systematic Analysis of Hybrid
  Linear Attention", as evidence that GatedDeltaNet is a promising related
  architecture option after the xLSTM downstream/base audit path.
- Verified paper details:
  - The paper is an architecture comparison of hybrid linear-attention models,
    not only a GatedDeltaNet paper.
  - It trains and releases `72` models: `36` at `340M` parameters on `20B`
    tokens and `36` at `1.3B` parameters on `100B` tokens.
  - It compares six linear-attention variants across five hybrid ratios.
  - Main practical recommendation: HGRN-2 or GatedDeltaNet with a
    linear-to-full-attention ratio between roughly `3:1` and `6:1`.
- Related primary paper:
  - arXiv `2412.06464`, "Gated Delta Networks: Improving Mamba2 with Delta
    Rule", ICLR 2025 camera-ready, frames GatedDeltaNet as combining adaptive
    gating and the delta rule to improve retrieval/long-context limitations of
    linear models such as Mamba2 and DeltaNet.
- Current interpretation for SALLM:
  - Do not interrupt the submitted 3-epoch xLSTM base retrain; it is already
    the fairness repair needed for current downstream claims.
  - Add GatedDeltaNet/HGRN-2 as the strongest next architecture-screen
    candidate after the xLSTM base/downstream results are defensible.
  - The likely experiment should be a base-screen first, not downstream first:
    fit a 125M-ish or closest feasible GatedDeltaNet/HGRN-2 shape, train on the
    same Mzansi tokenized corpus/budget, then run the same clean pretrain and
    task-conditioned generation-loss gates before spending on downstream.
  - Hybrid layers may be thesis-relevant, but should be scoped as a follow-up
    decision because they complicate the comparison axis: pure architecture
    family versus mixture of recurrent/linear and attention layers.
