## Cross-Cutting Findings

### Mamba can train, but not equally across tasks

Evidence against a blanket "Mamba is bad" conclusion:

- T2X recovered from about chrF `20` to chrF `32.18` after recipe recovery.
- News monolingual parity became strong after task-template correction.
- NER teacher-forced overfit and HPO screens show learning signals.

Evidence for real weaknesses:

- T2X remains far below LLaMA even after recovery.
- AfriHG improves with better recipes but remains well below LLaMA.
- POS/NER free-generation output control remains very weak.

### Hyperparameters matter substantially

Strongest evidence:

- T2X bqueawk-style recipe recovery materially improved Mamba.
- AfriHG monolingual recovered recipe improved over old weak baselines.
- AfriHG lower LR/no smoothing is worse for Xho, so not all "gentler" recipes
  help. The final Zul clean rerun is also worse.
- POS/NER do not inherit the T2X recipe; they need task-specific recipes and
  still struggle.

### Evaluation and metric hygiene mattered a lot

Confirmed bugs/confounds fixed or identified:

- News mono-vs-multi comparison was confounded by chat-template/task-pack
  differences.
- Built-in MasakhaNews tasks expected `headline_text`, while this repo uses
  `headline`/`text`.
- POS tag-sequence `doc_to_target` target-shape bug inflated metrics.
- Raw sample summarization was necessary to reveal overgeneration.
- Mamba generation batch cap and fallback were necessary because auto-batch
  probing at `16` can OOM; eval can fall back to `8` and still complete.
- Hydra override hygiene matters for full-model evals:
  - Do not set `eval_model.adapter=null`; `adapter` is typed `str`.
  - Do not override missing `peft_adapter` without `+`.

### Decoder-only constraint

All proposed fixes must remain decoder-only for the core comparison. Constrained
or guided output can be tested only as decoder-only decoding diagnostics or
matched method variants. It should not become an encoder, CRF, or classifier-head
architecture unless the research question explicitly changes.
