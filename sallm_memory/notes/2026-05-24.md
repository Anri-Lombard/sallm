## 2026-05-24 14:44 SAST - xLSTM hyperparameter research and implementation-risk read

  - Hugging Face Transformers xLSTM docs;
  - Beck et al. (2025), "xLSTM 7B".
- Main literature takeaways:
  - paper 125M-ish recipe is closer to embedding dim `768`, `24` blocks,
    `4` heads / head dim `384`, context length `2048`, batch size `256`,
    peak LR `3e-3` for 15B-token scaling or `1.5e-3` for 300B-token scaling;
  - paper training uses AdamW betas `(0.9, 0.95)`, epsilon `1e-5`, grad clip
    `1.0`, warmup `750`, cosine decay to 10% of peak LR, weight decay `0.1`
    excluding embeddings/biases/LayerNorm;
  - for 125M, paper notes sLSTM positions `[3, 20]`, effectively `11:1`
    mLSTM:sLSTM, and no positional encoding.
- Local repo mismatch:
  - current `src/conf/base/xlstm_125m.yaml` uses hidden `768`, 12 layers,
    context `512`, LR `4e-4`, warmup `2000`, weight decay `0.01`, tied
    embeddings;
  - local Transformers `4.57.3` parameter count for that config is about
    `135.37M`, within the repo's `120M`-`140M` bound but not a strict 125M;
  - closer strict shapes under SALLM vocab/tied embeddings include
    hidden `736` / 12 layers at `126.90M`, hidden `704` / 12 layers at
    `118.68M`, and hidden `672` / 16 layers at `130.86M`.
- Recommendation:
  - before a full xLSTM compute block, run a very small HF xLSTM viability
    check and, if practical, an official-vs-HF shape/parity smoke check;
  - do not require a full official xLSTM training path before downstream work,
    because that risks repeating the official-Mamba rabbit hole;
  - choose a primary SALLM xLSTM recipe deliberately: either strict 125M
