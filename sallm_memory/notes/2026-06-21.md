    worth only a small FLA-based hybrid feasibility screen unless the team
    explicitly chooses it over the stronger low-resource HGRN2/BabyHGRN path.
  - Evidence in favor: GDN combines Mamba2-style decay with DeltaNet targeted
    memory edits, and GDN/GDN-2 report stronger retrieval/long-context and LM
    results than nearby Mamba2/DeltaNet/KDA/Mamba-3 baselines. Qwen3-Next and
    Kimi Linear both support the broader hybrid-linear-attention direction.
  - Evidence against immediate full run: no direct South African-language or
    low-resource morphology result; strongest published sample-efficiency
    evidence among candidates still belongs to BabyHGRN/HGRN2; SALLM currently
    has no FLA/GDN dependency or config; official GDN code releases no
    pretrained weights and flags evaluation inconvenience/prompt sensitivity.
  - Do not promote to `sallm_progress.md`: this is architecture-recommendation
    research, not a landed experiment result.
